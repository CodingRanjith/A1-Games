# External 3D assets

All shipped 3D models in this project are **Kenney** packs under **CC0 1.0**.

They were downloaded on **2026-09-16** from Kenney’s OpenGameArt uploads (same files Kenney publishes at kenney.nl). They are **not** from Dr. Driving, Google Images, YouTube, or other games.

| Folder | Pack | Official page | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| models/cars, models/traffic, models/environment/props | Car Kit 3.1 | https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit | CC0 |
| models/roads | City Kit Roads 2.1 | https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads | CC0 |
| models/buildings/commercial | City Kit Commercial 2.1 | https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial | CC0 |
| models/buildings/suburban, models/environment | City Kit Suburban 2.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban | CC0 |

Per-folder records: see each `ASSET_LICENSE.md`.

Commercial use: allowed. Modification: allowed. Attribution: not required.

Nature Kit was **not** copied in full (too large for mobile). Trees come from the suburban kit instead.

Sketchfab / CGTrader / TurboSquid models were not used: many are paid or license-unclear. Anything without a confirmed license would be marked LICENSE REVIEW REQUIRED and omitted.
